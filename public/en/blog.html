Buying guides for more useful price comparisons

Reviewed by the Lumu team · September 30, 2026

A price comparison starts with matching products and ends with checking the cost of the purchase. These guides help you ask the right questions at each step.

Choose your next purchase

Use the phone guide to separate unlocked devices from carrier promotions. The laptop guide explains how model numbers, memory, screens and warranties affect comparisons. The headphone checklist helps you define what you need before sorting by price. The PC game key guide covers activation platforms, editions and regional restrictions.

Before calling something a deal

Confirm the same model, condition, seller and quantity. Add shipping and any known fees, then check tax at checkout. Record when you observed the price. A crossed-out price or a percentage badge alone does not prove savings.

Our approach

These are comparison checklists, not rankings based on lab testing or a live inventory feed. We do not publish invented prices or imply that a product is available because a listing exists. When retailer policies differ by seller or item, check the actual listing and checkout terms.
