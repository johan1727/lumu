Start with the exact product

Include the model, storage or capacity, and whether you want a new, refurbished, or open-box item. A lower price is useful only when the listings describe comparable products.

For example, “iPhone 16 128GB unlocked new” is more specific than “cheap iPhone.” For a laptop, include the manufacturer's model number, memory and storage.

Know what a result tells you

Product match

Review the original listing title and variant. Missing specifications do not prove that two products are identical.

Price and currency

A listed price is different from the final checkout total. Shipping, taxes, membership pricing and seller fees may need confirmation.

Availability

Read the availability label. A product page found on the web is a reference, not proof that the item is in stock.

How to compare stores

  1. Search for a specific product in your market.
  2. Separate different models and conditions.
  3. Open promising listings and check the seller, return terms, delivery and checkout total.
  4. Choose after confirming the details that matter to your purchase.

Source coverage and freshness vary. Lumu does not guarantee the lowest price across every retailer.

Questions before you buy

Does Lumu sell or ship the products?

No. Purchases happen on the retailer's site, where the seller confirms the order and its terms.

Are every price and stock level verified?

No. Results may contain incomplete information. Labels identify unknown cost or availability; confirm them in the store.

Does a link earn Lumu a commission?

Some links may. Affiliate commission does not determine the final offer order. A link does not guarantee that a partnership or promotion is active.
